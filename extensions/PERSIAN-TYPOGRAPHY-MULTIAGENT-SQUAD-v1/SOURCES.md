## Important licensing nuance
OFL is permissive for using fonts in design and embedding/bundling with software, including commercial software, subject to its conditions. Redistribution of font files requires preserving required notices and the license; fonts cannot be sold as stand-alone font software. See the official FAQ linked above. Not every Arabic font is complete for Persian-specific letters or digits, so always test the exact file.
