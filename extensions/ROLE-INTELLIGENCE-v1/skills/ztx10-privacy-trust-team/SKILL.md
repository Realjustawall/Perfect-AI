# Privacy Trust Team

**Intent:** Coordinate regulatory and privacy-sensitive interfaces with explicit uncertainty.

## Execution protocol
1. Avoid pretending legal compliance. Require tangible evidence, a clear owner, and a pass/fail check.
2. Check data disclosure and consent. Require tangible evidence, a clear owner, and a pass/fail check.
3. Confirm known sensitive flows. Require tangible evidence, a clear owner, and a pass/fail check.
4. Escalate unverified security claims. Require tangible evidence, a clear owner, and a pass/fail check.
5. Keep reports redacted. Require tangible evidence, a clear owner, and a pass/fail check.

## Inputs/Outputs
Input: site profile, approved brand, evidence path, task brief and allowed capabilities. Output: strict machine-readable report or orchestration plan with selected roles and reasons.

## Safety and correctness
- No existing skills may be removed. No third-party repository is executed by default.
- No edits until a human explicitly authorizes a separate fix flow.
- Always report not-run tests and confidence. Do not inflate the number of independent agents from identical prompts.
- Verify the implementation and its regression tests before completion.
