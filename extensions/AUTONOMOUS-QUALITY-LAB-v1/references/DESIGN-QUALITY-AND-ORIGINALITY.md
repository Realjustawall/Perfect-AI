# Evidence-based critique and originality

Aesthetic guidance cannot be reduced to pixel metrics. Assess typographic hierarchy, spatial rhythm, brand grounding, meaningful asymmetry, restrained gradients, task clarity and motion personality. Flag lazy identical three-column card grids when there is no content rationale. Preserve deliberate minimalist/monochrome designs where appropriate. Each issue must reference a route and screenshot and propose a concrete edit; criticisms should not force arbitrary decoration.

Maintain a design decision log with alternatives considered, constraints and why a style was accepted. Run advocate and critic independently; arbiter must preserve non-broken strengths.
